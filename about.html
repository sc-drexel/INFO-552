Sarah Cooke, MSc - About

from Arts For Life Board of Directors page

From the musical page to the digital stage, Sarah Cooke is an advocate for creative entrepreneurship and strategic communication with a focus on living local.

She pursued her education through her Bachelor of Arts in Music and Interdisciplinary Minor in Humanities from Furman University and her Master of Science in Entrepreneurship & Innovation from the University of Edinburgh Business School.

Sarah founded her blog and business, Strikes of Luck, in 2015 and specializes in content creation and digital marketing management for companies in the arts, literary, and technology spaces.

Sarah’s excitement for Arts For Life comes from her combined focus on art as a means for solace and her interest in supporting pediatric hospitals, specifically in her connection with facility dog programs.

She enjoys traveling from South Carolina to Scotland and beyond, discovering serendipity in her daily life.

Between these international adventures, Sarah searches Instagram for fun lattes at independent coffee shops and peruses Little Free Libraries for romance novels.

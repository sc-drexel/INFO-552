Philly Pen Pals - About

If you’re here... you’re a newbie to the world of romance novels, a novice at writing them, or a debut/established author ready to find your audience!

I’m Sarah, a lifelong reader and writer. I was introduced to romance in 2020, like so many of us on Bookstagram. While I’ve read through so many genres over the years, the HEA quickly became my absolute favorite.

As an avid Hallmark movie enthusiast as well (I’ve watched exactly 458 of them to this date, Monday, June 8, 2026) (and if you aren’t familiar with the Hallmark movie app, welcome to my future TED Talk), I’m in love with love. I wanted to create a space here with Philly Pen Pals that encompasses all of that love in a pretty pink format.

Whether you’re looking for authors, bookstores, events, and more, we have it all! And we’re not afraid to fall in love with YOU!

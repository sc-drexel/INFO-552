Philly Pen Pals - Membership

Want to join the Philly Pen Pals? Now’s your chance!

You can choose between a monthly (Pen Pal Plus) or an annual (Pen Pal Premium) membership.

---

Pen Pal Plus (charged every month on the 1st) - $10

- Includes two events a year, one writing workshop, and more to come!

---

Pen Pal Premium (charged every year on January 1st) - $100 (discounted $20)

- Includes four events a year, two writing workshops, and more to come!

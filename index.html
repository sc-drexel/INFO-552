Professional Philosophy Statement

As a hopeful library and information service professional, I see the role as a lighthouse. A lighthouse is a literal beacon of both bright light and directional information. This profession offers a bright light, a place for safety, and a sense of hope for people to find their place. The directional information offered is in the way that library and information service professionals provide resources and services to the people that they help in any way that they can.

I feel equipped to contribute to this profession because I have a deep appreciation and admiration for the world that opens up when people can discover themselves and their paths. I want to offer a seamless process to people who do not otherwise have that opportunity without the physical space of a library, the digital space of a chatroom or Zoom session, or a hybrid experience in between. There are so many ways to reach people these days, and I want to be able to provide a multitude of views of the world for them.

Philly Pen Pals - Welcome!

Squiggling and Swooning through Philadelphia, Pennsylvania

I’m your host and guide, Sarah, and I’m ready to delve into the world of romance for everyone!

Whether you are a contemporary, historical, monster, or any other sub-genre of romance author and/or admirer, I hope you will find a home here. I wanted to put together this website in order to collate all of the fun and frolic in Philly and the surrounding areas. This site is a space for writers and readers to connect, for friends to become members, and for romance supporters to flourish!

Thanks for being here, and I hope you find the HEA that you’re looking for in Philly Pen Pals!
